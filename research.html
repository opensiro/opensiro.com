02 / How it works ↖

Large models build.
Small models solve.

We evaluate small language models (SLMs). In the proposed workflow, a frontier AI system generates training data and transfers skills to a small model. That student model then takes the ARCTIC test.

A shared setup

Evaluation task samples stay hidden from the builder. It receives a public data-format example, task-category tags, and resource limits. After submission, accuracy by tag and the submission result guide the next iteration.

85 visual puzzles

ARCTIC-0 is an open archive of 85 ARC-style tasks with training examples, reference answers, and skill tags. The tasks explore patterns, structure, objects, and missing pieces across four difficulty levels.

What is available today

The archive and task editor are available now. The evaluator, feedback protocol, hosted runtime, and public leaderboard are still in development.

TIC, the Transfer & Induction Core, is the taxonomy used to describe the skills each task tests.
